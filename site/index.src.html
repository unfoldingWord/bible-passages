Bible Passages

Every passage of the Bible, divided into stories and the short segments a translator records, each with a plain title in the translator's own language. It lets a translator find the place in the story without chapter and verse.

Reviewers wanted

We need people who read Spanish, French or Hindi to review short Bible section titles for Titus. Two AI models each translated the 33 English Titus titles into 9 languages. Before we choose which model translates the other 65 books, a person who reads the language needs to check them: keep a title as it is, or change it, and note roughly how long it took. You will not be told which model wrote which title.

For each language that is 66 short titles (33 from each model). If you can help, or know someone who reads Arabic, Chinese, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian or Swahili and could, reply on the Zulip thread 86 Servant.Bible › Need: Bible Passages dataset. The plan is in #64.

Where it stands
  • Titus is built end to end on main: structure (8 pericopes, 25 segments), English segment titles, key-term glossaries in 9 languages, and a Chinese (zh-Hans) key-term list drafted by one model and checked by another, waiting for a Chinese reader (#51). The release path passed a dry run on GitHub (#35); no release has been tagged yet.
  • All 66 books are next. The code is merged (#40, #52); the generated structure for every book (3,016 pericopes, 13,770 segments, both counts re-derived by a second script) and glossaries for the 59 books with released word links are in review (#37, #62).
  • Titus is translated by both models (#58, measured 29 Oct): 297 titles each, no automatic-check failures. The other model disagreed with 10 of Gemini 3.8 Flash's translations and 5 of Claude Sonnet 5.5's; Gemini costs about half as much per title. The choice rule puts quality first, measured as reviewer changes (ADR 0002), so the choice waits on the review above.
  • Decisions are recorded (ADR 0003): v0.1 is a gated subset; pericopes come from BSB; segment titles are explanatory comments; single-segment pericopes reuse their title (16,523 English titles); v0.1 metadata is key terms, figures of speech and anything already in the data (sizing in #44). Keys are spans and never move once released (ADR 0001). Open: decision 8 (chunk source), the content team's call. See Decisions.

    Decisions · pick, say why, and the decider records it

    Decisions before data

    Pick the option you'd choose and add a reason. A pick is input; only a recorded decision counts. The requirements owner decides product questions, the content team decides the chunk source, and the DRI decides delivery questions. T1–T6 are Titus segment titles where the checking model disagreed with the drafting model (PR #16); the other 19 passed the check.     Review · one title at a time

    Does this title fit its passage?

    Read the passage, then approve its title, change the wording, or skip it. A maintainer turns reviews into a pull request on the repository, where your reviewer name is public. Your own name is never stored.
