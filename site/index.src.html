Bible Passages

Every passage of the Bible, divided into stories and the short segments a translator records, each with a plain title in the translator's own language. It lets a translator find the place in the story without chapter and verse.

Reviewers wanted

We need someone who reads Latin American Spanish or French to review short Bible section titles for Titus. Two AI models each translated the 33 English Titus titles into 9 languages. For each title, a reviewer keeps a model's wording, picks the other one, or writes their own, and we note roughly how long it took. You will not be told which model wrote which title. It is 33 titles, each with two wordings to choose from; the Hindi review took about 16 minutes.

Saved so far: English, Brazilian Portuguese and Hindi, one reviewer each. The Hindi answers were recovered on 3 Nov. Unblinded on 3 Nov, Gemini's titles needed fewer changes than Claude's (20 against 28 over Portuguese and Hindi), but one reviewer per language is too few to choose on, so the choice waits for one more language (#64). If you can help, reply on the Zulip thread 86 Servant.Bible › Need: Bible Passages dataset and we will send you the review page.

Where it stands
  • Go/no-go 1 was decided on 3 Nov, a day early (ADR 0005): v0.1.0 carries English titles for all 66 books, drafted by one AI model and checked by another. A person's review of every title moves to 1.0, after the content team's read-through in Q1 2027. Titus is the reviewed sample. When the checking model disagrees, a third model sorts the dispute; no AI-written title is marked higher than ai-checked.
  • English pericope titles for all 66 books: 3,016, from the BSB section headings, re-derived by a second script with no mismatches (#200).
  • English segment titles for 6 books: Titus, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Timothy and Genesis. 735 of them are in the tC Mobile title files (counted on 4 Nov from the built files and again from the draft records). Genesis is the first long book: the checker agreed with 602 of 642 drafts; it cost $1.73 and took 23.5 minutes (#231). One Genesis title the checker accepted is wrong (GEN 9:11–13 drops the limit on the flood promise); it is listed for review (#232) and will be named in the release notes until a person fixes it.
  • The drafting prompt was tightened: titles stay within 60 characters (#202), name the readers instead of an unclear “they” or “you” (#216, #228), and long books draft in batches of up to 35 (#210, #213).
  • The Bible text now reads for all 66 books: 31,104 verses, the same as the versification, counted again by a separate script (#234). Three verses the BSB numbers but leaves blank (Matthew 17:21, Mark 11:26, Romans 16:24) get a fixed title, “Not in the oldest manuscripts”, decided on 4 Nov (#236, in review).
  • The third-model dispute check is built (#203, #217, #222), but none of its results are in the data yet. First the maintainer sample-checks 9 blind disputes from Colossians, 1 Thessalonians and 2 Timothy (the review sheet, #207). The whole-Bible English run waits on that check.
  • Structure for all 66 books: 3,016 pericopes and 13,770 segments, counted again on 4 Nov with Python and with jq. The chunk source is still provisional: the content team has not answered #4 (checked 4 Nov).
  • Not re-checked since 2 Nov: glossaries for 59 books in 9 languages (the other 7 wait for a release, #57); the Chinese key-term list for Titus, waiting for a Chinese reader (#31); Open Bible Stories questions O1 to O5, after v0.1 (#78). No release has been tagged (checked 4 Nov).

Health of the road to v0.1.0

One verdict for the v0.1.0 tag on 16 Nov, and one line for each gate in the plan. Each status comes from what was observed on the day stamped above; the stamp turns amber after two days, because health goes stale fast. Numbers say whether they were measured or estimated.

What would change the verdict

    How each gate is judged

    • Passed Its condition is met. A late pass says how late.
    • On track The work left fits before the date at measured rates, with room to spare, and waits on no one.
    • At risk It fits only if a named decision or input lands by a named day.
    • Behind It cannot pass by its date, even if everything pending lands now.
    • Not passed The date has gone and the condition is not met; the plan's fallback is in force.
    • Overall: Ahead if the release could move earlier; On target if every open gate is on track; At risk if any gate on the way to the tag is at risk; Behind if the tag date cannot hold.

      Rates per title

      • English segment titles, drafting and checking: measured on 5 books. Genesis, the first long book, cost $0.0027 a title ($0.0017 to draft, $0.0010 to check) and took 23.5 minutes for 642 titles (#231). Short books cost more a title: $0.0026 for 2 Timothy and $0.0041 for 1 Thessalonians. Philippians (29 Oct) cost $0.0021. See sizing.md.
      • The rest of the Bible in English (estimated from Genesis's rates): 12,709 segment titles in 60 books, about $34 and 7.8 hours of model time, one call after another. For all 13,507, sizing.md gives about $36 and 8.2 hours.
      • Translation: measured 29 Oct on Titus, 297 titles per model (#58). Translating plus the other model's check: $0.0025 a title for Gemini 3.8 Flash and $0.0021 for Claude Sonnet 5.5. Not yet scaled to the 148,707 strings of v0.1 (16,523 English titles × 9 languages); the run waits on the model choice (#64).
      • Review time per title: first measured 3 Nov. The Hindi blind review: 33 titles in 16.4 minutes, 29.9 s on average (median 17.0 s), one reviewer choosing between two wordings (ADR 0005). That is a lower bound for a read-through. At 29.9 s, one language's 16,523 titles is about 137 hours (estimated), against 46 at the 10 seconds assumed before. ADR 0005 moves that review to 1.0, so it is not on the path to v0.1.0.
