## Watch items

- **Review hours dominate.** 165,230 strings at v0.1 (16,523 English titles
  after decision 12, × 10 languages). Even at an assumed 10 seconds a string
  that is about 459 hours of reading _(estimated; no review has been timed;
  recomputed 2026-11-02, superseding 167,860 strings and 466 hours, which
  predate decision 12)_. The dataset request says not every title needs human
  review before launch; which ones do is a decision, not a sizing detail.
- Not verified: how complete or reviewed each Translation Words translation
  is.
