# zh-Hans names check

A check that zh-Hans key-term headwords for proper names (`names/*`) agree
with the Chinese Union Version (CUV). Raised on #53 from the bench review of
PR #51; [glossaries.md](glossaries.md), "Not done", lists it. Exploration and
planning only: nothing here is built.

Observed 2026-11-02 at `main` `fdcbac4`.

separate `grep`/`awk` pass over the USFM); so was the 37 (`jq`, then
Python). The other rows are single `jq` or `cut` counts, each cheap to
repeat.

| Step   | Verdict                           | Evidence / justification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

| Break  | observed                          | (1) Licence: see Borrow. (2) Edition: the record's 革哩底 and `zh_cuv`'s 克里特 are two spellings of one place; which spellings the glossary follows (1919 original or New Punctuation) is a content decision, not a check. (3) FIA versus CUV: run over CUV Mark, 7 of the 37 capitalised FIA names have no hit for any form. 3 are real wording differences (无酵节 / 除酵节, 希律党人 / 希律一党的人, 叙利亚腓尼基 / 叙利腓尼基), 2 are form differences (U+2022 in 凯撒利亚•腓立比 against U+00B7; 加利利的海 against 加利利海), 1 is absent from Mark's wording (弥赛亚), and 1 is a parse artefact of the annotated Judea entry. FIA is binding (maintainer decision on #53), so a CUV check would have to exempt every term that names an FIA `reference`. |
| Beget  | skipped                           | The licence question can only be answered by the maintainer (or by the copyright agent through them); no team carries a zh-Hans names list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Bide   | waiting                           | Reason: in Titus the check covers 5 terms, and the Chinese reader (#31, not yet named) is to read all 93 ([glossaries.md](glossaries.md), "Not done"). Tripwire: before the zh-Hans key terms of a second book are drafted (#53's own gate). Outcomes then: (1) a written answer says the New Punctuation text is public domain or otherwise usable as an input under AGENTS.md, and the content team has chosen its spellings over the 1919 ones: Build. (3) No written answer yet: keep biding, and do not draft the next book's zh-Hans key terms until there is one.                                                                                              |
| Build  | not-yet                           | Only on outcome (1): pin one `zh_cuv` book file per drafted book; for each `names/*` term with no FIA `reference`, require one headword form in the CUV verse at every TWL reference (markers stripped, `·`/`•` normalised); record a mismatch as a gate the reader sees, not a hard failure. Traced to Breaks (2) and (3).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

Reversibility: forward = low; backward = low. Nothing is pinned or built; a
later check is one source entry and one gate.

## Recommendation

Bide. Do not pin `zh_cuv` now. For Titus, put the Crete spelling in front of
the Chinese reader as a question (革哩底 or 克里特), and let the reader's
review cover the other four names, which already agree with `zh_cuv`.

## Commands

Scratch scripts lived outside the tree; downloads went to their own empty
directories.

```sh
S=b993881167d730846cf2efcc1e0a1f58a3cd8b65
curl -s "https://git.door43.org/api/v1/catalog/search?lang=zh&limit=50" | jq -r '.data[]|[.full_name,.subject]|@tsv'
curl -s "https://git.door43.org/Door43-Catalog/zh_cuv/raw/commit/$S/manifest.yaml"   # rights: 'Public Domain'
curl -s "https://git.door43.org/Door43-Catalog/zh_cuv/raw/commit/$S/57-TIT.usfm"     # sha256 a78a9a3e…dde2
curl -s "https://git.door43.org/Door43-Catalog/zh_cuv/raw/commit/$S/42-MRK.usfm"     # sha256 4e2c8611…0d4
curl -s https://ebible.org/Scriptures/translations.csv | grep cmn                   # cmn-cu89s: public domain
jq -r '.terms|to_entries[]|select(.key|startswith("names/"))|[.key,.value.headword,(.value.reference//"-")]|@tsv' \
  data/glossary-drafts/zh-Hans/TIT.json
grep "/names/" .cache/sources/twl-en-tit.* | cut -f1,6
cat .cache/sources/twl-en-* | cut -f6 | grep -o '/names/[a-z0-9-]*' | sort -u | wc -l                # 352
cat .cache/sources/twl-en-* | cut -f6 | grep -o '/\(kt\|other\|names\)/[a-z0-9-]*' | sort -u | wc -l  # 938
```
