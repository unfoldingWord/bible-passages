How to read a row:

- **Verses (ULT)**: what the third model read (unfoldingWord/en_ult v91,
  CC BY-SA 4.0). The checker read BSB.
- **Checker on the draft**: the first check's criteria and reason. Its
  suggested title is left out here, as it was left out of the third
  model's input.
- **Kind**: `taste` replaces the draft if the checker agrees with the new
  title (**Checker on the title**); `factual` is only a proposal and stays
  `unreviewed`.
- **Status**: what the build would give the title.
- The last two columns are for the person: **yes** or **no**. "Keeps every
  main point" asks whether the third model's title keeps the main point of
  every verse. "Factual call correct" asks whether `taste` or `factual` is
  the right sort for the draft.
