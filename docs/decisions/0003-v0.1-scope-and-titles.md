The six disputed Titus segment titles (T1–T6, #16) were decided at the same

owner's own wording for 1:12–13 ("Titus must firmly correct false teachers")
and 2:6–8

## Decision 11 and T1, confirmed 29 Oct

On 28 Oct the pick for 11 was "all of it" (topics, key terms, figures of
speech, literary form, difficulty), which added work the 4 Nov go/no-go had
not sized, so it was confirmed first. On 29 Oct the requirements owner
narrowed it: not all of it, but more than key terms. v0.1 has key terms,
figures of speech, and whatever else is already in the data and does not
need to be created. Which other metadata qualifies is measured, not assumed
(#44).

For T1 the checking model noted that "them" did not say who. The
requirements owner changed the wording to "Titus must firmly correct false
teachers".

## Why

These were the proposals on servant.bible#1 and the site, except 12, where
the smaller set was chosen: fewer strings to translate and review, and the
two titles would describe the same verses.
