| #   | Question                      | Decision                                                                                                           |

The six disputed Titus segment titles (T1–T6, #16) were decided at the same
time: the drafts for 1:14, 1:15–16, 3:9–11 and 3:15, and the requirements
owner's own wording for 1:12–13 ("Titus must firmly correct them") and 2:6–8
("Titus instructs younger men"). They are recorded as `review` entries in
`data/drafts/en/TIT.json` with status `human-reviewed`.

## Why

These were the proposals on servant.bible#1 and the site, except 12, where
the smaller set was chosen: fewer strings to translate and review, and the
two titles would describe the same verses.

## Not decided here

- **11, metadata in v0.1.** The pick was "all of it" (topics, key terms,
  figures of speech, literary form, difficulty), against the proposal of key
  terms only. That adds work the 4 Nov go/no-go has not sized, so it is
  confirmed with the requirements owner before it is recorded.
- **8, chunk source.** The content team's call. The requirements owner's pick
  (the UFW chunk files) is input to it.
